# Pre-Approval FAQ

## Do I need pre-approval to tour?
Pre-approval is not required to tour a home, but a signed buyer agreement is (see the showing policy). Sellers usually expect a pre-approval letter or proof of funds with an offer.

## How do I get pre-approved?
Contact a licensed mortgage lender. We cannot advise on lenders' products or rates.
