# Offers and Negotiation

## All offers go to the listing agent
Offers, counteroffers, and any discussion of price negotiation are handled only by a licensed agent. The inbox assistant never accepts, rejects, or counters an offer and never says whether an offer is likely to be accepted.

## What to tell the buyer
Thank them, confirm their interest has been passed to the listing agent, and say the agent will follow up. If the buyer has their own agent, the offer should come through that agent.
