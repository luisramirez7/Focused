# Email Safety

## Never send sensitive data by email
Please never email credit card numbers, bank account or routing numbers, or Social Security numbers. Harborview Realty will never ask for them by email.

## Wire fraud warning
Always verify wiring instructions by phone with the escrow company using a number you look up independently. If a buyer has already sent sensitive data, advise them not to send more and to contact their bank if they are concerned.
