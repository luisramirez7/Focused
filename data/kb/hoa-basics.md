# HOA Basics

## What HOA fees cover
Homeowners association (HOA) dues vary by community. They commonly cover exterior maintenance, landscaping, shared amenities, and reserves. The exact amount for a listing is shown on its listing record; what the fee covers is described in the listing description and the HOA documents.

## HOA documents
Buyers receive the HOA's governing documents, budget, and recent meeting minutes during the transaction. Special assessments, if any, are disclosed in those documents.

## Homes without an HOA
If a listing shows no HOA fee, the home is not part of an HOA.
