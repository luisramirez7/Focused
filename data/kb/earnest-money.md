# Earnest Money

## What it is
Earnest money is a deposit made when an offer is accepted, typically 1–3% of the price, held by the escrow company.

## How it is paid
Earnest money is paid directly to the escrow company using the wire or check instructions they provide by phone verification. Harborview Realty never collects deposits by email, and buyers should never send card, bank, or Social Security numbers by email.
