# Showing Policy

## Who can tour
Harborview Realty requires a **signed written buyer agreement** on file before any agent-accompanied private showing. This follows common practice after the 2024 NAR settlement. If a buyer has not signed one, we cannot book a private showing yet; their assigned agent (or the front desk) will send the agreement for e-signature first. Open houses do not require an agreement.

## Showing hours
Private showings are available Monday through Saturday between 10:00 AM and 6:00 PM Pacific time. No private showings on Sundays.

## Notice
Showings must be requested at least 24 hours in advance so the seller can be notified. Requests for times in the past or with less than 24 hours' notice cannot be booked; offer the next available slots instead.

## Confirmation
A showing is only confirmed after a Harborview coordinator approves it. Never tell a buyer a showing is confirmed before approval.

## Pending and sold homes
Homes that are pending or sold are not available for showings. A buyer may ask their agent about submitting a backup offer on a pending home.
