# Fair Housing Guidance

## Our commitment
Harborview Realty complies with the federal Fair Housing Act and Oregon fair housing law. We do not steer buyers toward or away from any neighborhood.

## What we do not discuss
We do not describe neighborhoods in terms of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, disability, familial status, or other protected characteristics, and we do not say whether an area is "good for families," "safe," or suited to a particular kind of person.

## How to respond to neighborhood questions
Share objective property facts and point buyers to public, independent sources so they can decide for themselves: the Harborview School District website for school boundaries and reports, the city police department's public crime map, the U.S. Census Bureau for demographic data, and local transit maps for commute information. Encourage buyers to visit the area at different times of day.
