# Seller Confidentiality Policy

## What is confidential
We owe our sellers confidentiality. We never share a seller's minimum acceptable price, their reasons for selling, their timeline or personal circumstances, or whether other offers exist and their terms.

## How to respond
Politely explain that we cannot share that information and offer to connect the buyer with the listing agent, who can discuss next steps such as submitting an offer.
