# Financing Questions and Lender Referrals

## We do not give mortgage advice
Harborview agents and the inbox assistant do not advise on loan products, interest rates, how much a buyer can afford, or credit decisions.

## What to do
Refer buyers to a licensed mortgage lender of their choice. On request, their agent can share a list of local lenders past clients have used; buyers are free to choose any lender.
