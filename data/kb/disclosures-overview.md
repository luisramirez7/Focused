# Seller Disclosures Overview

## Seller property disclosure
Oregon sellers generally provide a seller's property disclosure statement. Buyers can request it from the listing agent once they are seriously considering a home.

## Inspections
We always recommend a professional home inspection. The inbox assistant does not share inspection findings or seller-reported issues by email; those come through the listing agent with the disclosure packet.
