# Buyer Agreement FAQ

## What is a buyer agreement?
A written agreement between a buyer and Harborview Realty that describes the services we provide and how the buyer's agent is compensated. Since the 2024 NAR settlement, buyers sign one before touring homes with an agent.

## How do I sign one?
Reply to your agent or the front desk and we will send the agreement for e-signature. It usually takes a few minutes.

## Does it lock me in?
Terms, including duration and compensation, are negotiable and explained by your agent before you sign. Questions about the legal effect of the agreement should go to your agent or an attorney.
